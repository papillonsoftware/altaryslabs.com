# UI-001 - Two-surface system

**Type** STORY | **Mode** BOOTSTRAP | **Branch** `feat/surfaces-claires` | **Base** `refonte-multipages` at `480d4d4`

**Decisions** D028 to D032, plus D036, D037, D039, D040 and D041 in
`docs/DECISIONS.md`. D033 to D035 are reserved by `SITE-FIX-001` on another
branch; the gap is deliberate.

## Goal

The July 2026 design handoff turns the site from all-navy to an alternation of
cream and navy sections. This item installs the light surface family and the
rules that go with it, **without flipping the default background**, so nothing
changes visually except the contrast corrections. The switch itself is UI-002.

## What ships here

- `tokens.css`: the light surface family, the navy veils, the two shadows.
  Nothing removed.
- `global.css`: the `.section--light` scope that recolours existing components
  without duplicating them, plus base form-control styles for the future FORM
  item. Neither is used by any page yet.
- Eleven contrast corrections across seven components and `global.css`,
  of which nine actually render.
- Light-surface coverage for the eight component-owned classes and for
  `.btn-primary`, added after the R2 review measured them between 1.02:1 and
  2.91:1 once the foundation is woken up. See D039 and D040.
- `CLAUDE.md`: palette section rewritten, two accessibility rules added, product
  name corrected, visual reference repointed to the live prototypes.

## Contrast corrections

Eleven occurrences, all the same defect: small text in translucent gold or
ivory. Ratios measured against the actual background each element sits on.

| Element | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `global.css` `.section-label` | `--gold-65`, 3.49:1 | `--gold-light`, 10.03:1 |
| `AboutContent` `.leadership-label` | `--gold-65`, 3.06:1 | `--gold-light`, 7.87:1 |
| `ApproachCard` `.approach-label` | `--gold-65`, 3.06:1 | `--gold-light`, 7.87:1 |
| `Footer` `.footer-legal p` | `--ink-25`, 2.09:1 | `--ink-60`, 5.90:1 |
| `Footer` `.footer-legal-links a` | `--ink-40`, 3.61:1 | `--ink-60`, 5.90:1 |
| `ModuleGrid` `.module-label` | `--ink-25`, 2.09:1 | `--ink-60`, 5.90:1 |
| `Header` `.nav-menu-tagline` | `--ink-40`, 3.46:1 | `--ink-60`, 5.22:1 |
| `PageScaffold` `.breadcrumb li` | `--ink-25`, 2.09:1 | `--ink-60`, 5.90:1 |
| `PageScaffold` `.breadcrumb a` | `--ink-40`, 3.61:1 | `--ink-60`, 5.90:1 |
| `LegalPage` `.legal-updated` | `--ink-25` | `--ink-60`, **inert** |
| `LegalPage` `.legal-prevails` | `--ink-40` | `--ink-60`, **inert** |

**The two `LegalPage` rules never applied in the first place.** `.legal-body
:global(p)`, written for slotted content, has higher specificity and also
captures the component's own two paragraphs, which therefore render in
`--muted` at 14.5px. Their real contrast is good, by accident. The token is
changed anyway so the rule cannot wake up at 2.09:1 the day the specificity
collision is fixed. **That collision is a latent defect reported at the done
gate and left to a follow-up item**, since fixing it would change the rendering
of both legal pages, which is outside this item's scope.

`Header` `.nav-disclosure` keeps `--ink-40`: it is an icon, not text, and 3.61:1
clears the 3:1 non-text threshold.

## Verification method

Rather than eyeballing screenshots, the computed styles of **every element** on
six pages were captured before and after over the DevTools protocol and diffed:
colour, background, border colour and font size. Element counts are identical
and the only differences are the corrections above. No layout, background,
border or type-scale change anywhere.

## Prototype survey, so later items need not redo it

Read through `DesignSync` on project `53d1c228-d274-4df3-8022-1a427dd96c15`, at
the **root**, on 31 July 2026: 13 pages plus `Header` and `Footer`.

**The structure of every page is unchanged.** What changes is the surface. Six
structural deltas exist:

1. **Footer** goes from 4 columns to 5. A `Legal` column appears with the legal
   notice, the privacy policy and Contact; `Entreprise` shrinks to About; the
   bottom legal-links row and "Tous droits reserves" disappear.
2. **Header** becomes light and sticky, `#FBF9F4` at 94 percent with a blur,
   white dropdown panels with a heavy shadow.
3. **Contact** finally has a full prototype: six fields, an interest select and
   a success state. **No Turnstile widget is drawn**, which is a gap in the
   mockup, not a decision.
4. **The two legal pages** now have prototypes. Their copy is locked, see D032.
5. **Papillon HR Suite** alternates light, dark, light: white feature cards, the
   navy rollout section kept, white module cards.
6. **PCS** loses its closing call-to-action band: hero, then footer.

**Colour system observed across all 13 pages**, uniform: `#F6F2E9` page,
`#FFFFFF` cards with `1px solid rgba(26,39,64,.08)` and a `0 1px 3px` shadow,
`#1A2740` headings, `#5B6472` body, `#B8791E` accents, navy gradient CTA band,
`#040C16` footer.

**Six points where the prototypes are wrong and the repository wins**: the RCCM
is written `CI-ABI` throughout; "Papillon Corporate Finance" loses its "Suite"
in five files but keeps it in `contact.dc.html`; the HR page reintroduces "MVP"
and hardens the OHADA coverage claim to "17 countries by 2028"; the legal copy
regresses; the English hero was left on the old positioning while the French one
changed; and several colours fail WCAG AA.

## Out of scope

The default background flip, the header, the footer, any page, any dictionary.
Recorded for `I18N-001`: the consulting service is renamed "Conseil IT, RH &
Corporate Finance" and "IT, HR & Corporate Finance Consulting"; the English role
becomes "Founder and Chairman"; the hero adopts the handoff wording with
**"Zones" in the plural** and an English line the handoff never wrote, "Your
technology partner for businesses across Africa." with "OHADA and CIMA regions".
