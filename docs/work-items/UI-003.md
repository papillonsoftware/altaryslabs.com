## Goal

Apply the updated `Footer.dc.html`, which reorganises the footer columns and
publishes contact details for the first time. That unblocks `PAGE-002`'s D069,
whose error state and no-JavaScript note were worded for a site with no channel
to name.

## What changed

**Footer columns**

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Entreprise: About | **Contact**: email, phone, contact form |
| 5 | Legal: Legal notice, Privacy policy, Contact | **Legal**: Legal notice, Privacy policy, **About** |

The Contact column leaves the `{ title, keys: PageKey[] }` abstraction the other
columns share: it mixes `mailto:`, `tel:` and one internal route, and forcing
those into a `PageKey` list would have meant inventing routes that do not exist.

**Contact details**

`CONTACT_EMAIL` and `CONTACT_PHONE` in `src/i18n/config.ts`, beside `SITE_URL`,
which is already a language-independent fact. `CONTACT_PHONE_HREF` is derived by
stripping spaces, so the dialled number cannot contradict the displayed one.
Only the labels are translated.

**D069 closed**

One shared sentence, `contact.fallbackText`, ends both the error panel and the
no-JavaScript note, followed by a `mailto:` link. The Contact page stops being a
dead end for the visitor whose send fails and for the one whose browser cannot
mint a Turnstile token.

## Out of scope

The email and phone are **not** added to the legal notice or to the JSON-LD
`Organization` block, though both would take them. That is an editorial and SEO
widening that deserves its own arbitration.

The footer taglines stay as they are. `I18N-FIX-001` owns them.
