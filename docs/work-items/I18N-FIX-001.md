## Scope changed after this document was first written

It was created to fix two keys, the two `footer.tagline` values. The round 1
review of `I18N-001` then found the same defect in four `meta.home.*` values and
burnt into the two Open Graph thumbnails, and raised both as blockers.

stopped at the footer; fixing only the footer next would have stopped one step
further along the same road. **The defect is one family and is swept as one.**

### Comments

`src/components/ServiceConsultingContent.astro` named the service in the spelling
D044 retired, the last surviving occurrence in `src/`.

The `en.ts` file header promised that OHADA and CIMA were "spelled out on first
use, applied consistently across every English string", which four bare acronyms
introduced here made false.

**The original block and one rewrite shipped false**, and `I18N-FIX-002` needed
two review rounds to stop writing more. The distinction matters here of all
places: `12025d9` created the block, it did not rewrite anything. This paragraph originally described the
second one, which enumerated the pages that expand the acronyms and the pages
that do not; both lists were wrong.

The comment is now written **as instructions rather than as a description of the
file**, and it carries no enumeration and no count. That is the form the four
false versions could not take: no build step reads a comment, so a description
drifts and nothing says so, while an instruction cannot be factually wrong. See
D119, which records the four and why the grammar was the cause.

## Three things a reader must not "fix"

1. **The titles say OHADA and CIMA, the descriptions say West and Central
   Africa.** That split is a founder ruling, D072, taken with the consequence
   stated. It is not an oversight.
2. **`home.heroTitle` keeps its capital in `entreprises Africaines`**, D074,
   against French grammar and against the review's recommendation. Deliberate.
3. **`en.ts:7` still contains `African markets`.** It is a header comment
   describing the file's target audiences, not user-facing copy.
