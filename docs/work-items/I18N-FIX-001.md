# I18N-FIX-001 - The footer tagline still carries the pre-handoff positioning

**Type** FIX | **Status** OPEN, not started | **Branch to create** `fix/tagline-pied-de-page`
**Base** `refonte-multipages`

**Decision** D063 in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Found while delivering `I18N-001`.

## The defect

`footer.tagline` is the one-line sentence under the logomark in the footer. It is
identical on all 26 pages. In both languages it is, almost word for word, the
hero title that `I18N-001` has just replaced.

| Key | Current value |
|---|---|
| `src/i18n/fr.ts` `footer.tagline` | `Solutions technologiques pour les entreprises d'Afrique, zones OHADA et CIMA.` |
| `src/i18n/en.ts` `footer.tagline` | `Enterprise software for African companies, across West and Central Africa.` |

Since `I18N-001` merged, every page therefore reads:

- top: `Votre partenaire technologique pour les entreprises Africaines.`
- bottom: `Solutions technologiques pour les entreprises d'Afrique...`

**The English pair is worse than repetitive, it is contradictory.** The hero now
says `OHADA and CIMA regions`; the footer still says `West and Central Africa`.
Those are not the same territory. D043 changed the hero precisely because the
handoff had left English on the old positioning, and the footer is the same trap
one level down.

## Why it was not fixed in `I18N-001`

Founder ruling, recorded in D063. `I18N-001` only applies rulings already taken.
New copy for these two lines is not covered by any ruling and has to be drafted
and validated, which is a different kind of work and a different kind of review.

## Scope

Two dictionary keys. No component, no page, no route, no `routes.ts`.

## What needs a founder ruling before any edit

**The replacement wording, in both languages.** Do not write it alone. The
sentence is a positioning statement on all 26 pages, which makes it editorial
direction, not implementation. Propose, then wait.

Two constraints the proposal must respect:

- French keeps the plural `zones OHADA et CIMA` (D042).
- English is a re-adaptation, not a translation (D014), and must not reintroduce
  `West and Central Africa` now that the hero has dropped it (D043).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The two `footer.tagline` values are replaced by founder-approved wording, and
   no other string changes.
2. No page states a different positioning in its hero and in its footer, in
   either language.
3. `npm run build` and `npm run check` are green.
4. The footer renders correctly at 360, 768 and 1440 px in both languages. The
   tagline sits in the first of five footer columns (D055), which is the
   narrowest place the sentence has to survive.

## Collision surface

`src/i18n/fr.ts` and `src/i18n/en.ts` only. Coordinate with any item editing the
same two files; `PAGE-002` does, in the Contact page keys.
