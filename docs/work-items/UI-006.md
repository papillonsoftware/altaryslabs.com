# UI-006 - Repaint the dark surfaces on the September 2026 handoff blues

- **Type**: STORY
- **Status**: delivered, awaiting review
- **Base**: `post-refonte`
- **Date raised**: 2026-09-08
- **Decision**: D136

## Problem

The founder updated the claude.ai/design project
`53d1c228-d274-4df3-8022-1a427dd96c15` and asked for the site to follow: the
hero and the footer read as too dark. The root prototypes now paint the dark
surfaces in blue rather than in near-black navy. Copy is untouched; this item
changes colour values only.

## Values taken from the handoff

Read from `index.dc.html` and `Footer.dc.html` at the project ROOT, never from
`design_handoff_altaryslabs_refonte/` (the frozen all-navy folder).

| Role | Was | Now | Token |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page-level dark background (hero, `.section--deep`) | `#07111e` | `#12478a` | `--navy-deep` |
| Cards on a dark background | `#1a2740` / `#101d30` | `#36639c` | `--navy-mid`, `--navy-card` |
| Call-to-action band gradient | `#1a2740` to `#0f1724` | `#1d3f6e` to `#0f2847` | new `--navy-cta`, `--navy` |
| Footer | `#040c16` | `#2c5c97` | `--navy-footer` |
| Body text on a dark background | `#9aa7be`, `#8b98af`, `#6b7a96` | `#ffffff` | `--muted`, `--muted-soft`, `--slate` |

Light surfaces are unchanged: the handoff still paints `#f6f2e9`, `#ffffff`,
`#1a2740`, `#5b6472`, `#b8791e` and `#8b5e1b`. `Header.dc.html` is unchanged
too, cream at `rgba(251,249,244,.94)`.

## The accessibility consequence, and how it is answered

Lighter backgrounds cost contrast. Eleven declarations fell under the 4.5:1 AA
floor for normal text. Ratios below are computed on sRGB relative luminance.

| Element | Colour before | On | Ratio | Now | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero subtitle, `.lede`, footer links, `.roadmap-intro` | `--muted` | `#12478a` / `#2c5c97` | 3.76 / 1.57 | `#ffffff` | 9.15 / 6.81 |
| Card body, `.card-text` | `--muted-soft` | `#36639c` | 2.11 | `#ffffff` | 6.14 |
| Footer tagline | `--slate` | `#2c5c97` | 1.57 | `#ffffff` | 6.81 |
| Footer legal line | `--ink-60` | `#2c5c97` | 3.08 | `--ink-100` | 5.34 |
| `.wave-text` | `--ink-80` | `#36639c` | 4.02 | `--ink-100` | 5.01 |
| Footer column titles | `--gold` | `#2c5c97` | 2.46 | `--gold-pale` | 5.52 |
| Footer link hover, `.logo--dark .logo-labs` | `--gold-light` | `#2c5c97` | 3.60 | `--gold-pale` | 5.52 |
| `.link-arrow`, `.wave-label` | `--gold-light` | `#36639c` | 3.24 | `--gold-pale` | 4.98 |
| `.hero-badge-text` | `--gold-light` | `#12478a` under a `--gold-10` veil | 4.45 | `--gold-pale` | 6.84 |
| `.btn-primary`, `.badge--solid`, `.skip-link`, `.nav-cta` | `--navy-deep` | fill `#c8922a` | would fall to 3.31 | new `--navy-ink` `#07111e` | 6.86 |

Three points are worth stating rather than leaving to be re-derived.

**The gold rule is now surface-dependent.** `--gold-light` still holds on
`--navy-deep` at 4.83:1, which is why the eyebrows, the section titles and the
italic emphasis of the hero keep the handoff's saturated gold. It fails on the
two lighter surfaces only, `--navy-card` and `--navy-footer`, and those two take
`--gold-pale`. The rule is written into `tokens.css` next to the three gold
values so the next session does not have to measure again.

**Four of those rows deviate from the handoff, deliberately.** `Footer.dc.html`
paints the column titles `#c8922a` (2.46:1) and the legal line
`rgba(255,255,255,.6)` (3.58:1). Both fail. This is the same divergence D029 and
D030 already recorded: on contrast the repository wins against the mockup.

**`--navy-deep` carried two roles that have now diverged.** It was both the page
background and the text laid on a gold fill. Repainting it blue would have taken
the label of every primary button from 6.86:1 to 3.31:1. The text role moves to
`--navy-ink`, frozen at the old `#07111e`, and is documented as never being a
background.

## Share previews

`bin/og_images` is rendered outside the Astro pipeline and copies token values
into its own constants. `MARINE` and `ATTENUE` follow `--navy-deep` and
`--muted`, and both PNGs are regenerated. Without this the share preview would
have stayed near-black while the site turned blue, and `--check` would not have
seen it: it compares the render to the constants in that file, not to
`tokens.css`. `OR_CLAIR` stays on "LABS" and on the italic, because the preview
paints on `--navy-deep`, where `--gold-light` clears the floor.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The five dark surfaces carry the handoff values. **Met**, read back from the
   built CSS: `--navy-deep:#12478a`, `--navy-card:#36639c`, `--navy-mid:#36639c`,
   `--navy-cta:#1d3f6e`, `--navy:#0f2847`, `--navy-footer:#2c5c97`.
2. No text falls under WCAG AA. **Met**: `bin/contrast_sweep` over the 26 built
   pages returns `AUCUN ECHEC AA`.
3. The sweep is proved to be live rather than vacuously green. **Met**: reverting
   `--muted` to `#9aa7be` and rebuilding returns **82 failures**; restoring it
   returns zero again.
4. The 22 elements the sweep leaves unmeasured, all on the gradient band, are
   checked by hand against its lighter end `#1d3f6e`: `.cta-title` in `--ivory`
   at 9.88:1, `.btn-secondary` in `--gold-light` at 5.58:1, and the `.btn-primary`
   gold fill at 3.82:1 against the band, above the 3:1 of SC 1.4.11. **Met.**
5. No copy is changed. **Met**: the diff touches colour declarations, two
   `bin/og_images` constants and the two PNGs.
6. `npm run build` passes. **Met**, 26 pages.

## Follow-up left open

`--muted`, `--muted-soft` and `--slate` now hold the same value. They encoded
three levels of grey that only ever separated on near-black; on the handoff
blues the handoff itself collapses them to white. Merging the three into one
token touches around ten declarations across five files and is left to its own
item, recorded in `docs/BACKLOG.md`.
