# UI-005 - Regenerate the Open Graph images on the new logomark

- **Type**: STORY
- **Status**: **DONE**, delivered by UI-FIX-003 on 2026-08-07
- **Base**: `refonte-multipages`
- **Date raised**: 2026-08-07

## Outcome

Closed by `UI-FIX-003`. Review round 2 of UI-004 ruled that tracking a blocker
does not discharge it, so the work was pulled forward rather than scheduled.

Both images are now generated by `bin/og_images` rather than hand-drawn, from
the real fonts and the D086 logomark, with their copy read out of `fr.ts` and
`en.ts`. See D101.

## Problem, as originally raised

`public/og-image.png` and `public/og-image-en.png` embedded the pre-UI-004
logomark: the thin diamond (1.5 outline, 2.4 ligature, `r=2.2` dots) and the
DM Sans 300 wordmark. Since D086 the site carries the thicker diamond.

These two files are what LinkedIn, WhatsApp and X render when someone shares any
of the 26 pages. Until they were regenerated, every share preview showed a logo
the site no longer used.

## Two errors in this document, corrected before closing it

Both were found by review round 2 and are recorded here rather than silently
edited away.

- The type was `CHR`. The decision tree in `docs/AI_Development_Workflow.md`
  turns on "does a visitor see a difference?", and a share preview is the first
  thing a visitor sees. It is a `STORY`. A reviewer taking a `CHR` would have
  been excused the visual check on an item that is nothing but an image.
- The note referred to a constant `OG_IMAGES`. It is `OG_DEFAULT`, in
  `BaseLayout.astro:20`.

## Acceptance criteria, as met

1. Both images carry the D086 diamond. **Met.**
2. Dimensions and file names unchanged, so `BaseLayout.astro` needed no edit.
   **Met**, 1200x630 for both.
3. Each stays within its original weight budget. **Met**, 66 KB against 70 and
   59 KB before.
4. The FR and EN variants differ only where they already differed. **Superseded**:
   the review found the English copy carried a retired positioning, and the
   French one did too. Both now read from the dictionaries. Freezing the existing
   difference would have preserved the defect.
