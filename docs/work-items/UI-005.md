# UI-005 - Regenerate the Open Graph images on the new logomark

- **Type**: CHR
- **Status**: NOT STARTED, deferred from UI-004
- **Base**: `refonte-multipages`
- **Date raised**: 2026-08-07

## Problem

`public/og-image.png` and `public/og-image-en.png` embed the pre-UI-004
logomark: the thin diamond (1.5 outline, 2.4 ligature, `r=2.2` dots) and the
DM Sans 300 wordmark. Since D086 the site itself carries the thicker diamond and
a 700 wordmark.

These two files are what LinkedIn, WhatsApp and X render when someone shares any
of the 26 pages. Until they are regenerated, every share preview shows a logo the
site no longer uses. That is precisely the surface where an Ivorian executive
forms a first impression before ever reaching the site.

## Why it was deferred

They are rasters. Regenerating them faithfully needs the design source, not a
hand-rolled redraw, and inventing an approximation would have put a third
variant of the mark into circulation.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Both images carry the D086 diamond and the 700 wordmark.
2. Dimensions and file names are unchanged, so `BaseLayout.astro` needs no edit
   and no cache key moves.
3. Each stays under the weight budget that made them acceptable originally.
4. The FR and EN variants differ only where they already differed.

## Notes

- `BaseLayout.astro` maps them at `OG_IMAGES = { fr: '/og-image.png', en: '/og-image-en.png' }`.
- The reference geometry is in `src/components/Logo.astro` and, standalone, in
  `public/assets/favicon.svg`.
