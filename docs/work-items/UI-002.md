# UI-002 - Switch the site to the light surface system

**Type** STORY | **Status** **DONE**, merged on 2026-08-07 as `1291501`
**Base** `refonte-multipages`

The status said "OPEN, not started" until round 3 of `I18N-FIX-002`, a month
after the branch shipped, while `docs/BACKLOG.md` listed it under Done. Nothing
reconciles the two; `SITE-FIX-014` makes that check mechanical.

Decisions to reserve at execution time, after grepping every open branch.

## Goal

`UI-001` installed the light surface family and left it dormant. This item
performs the switch: the document background becomes cream, dark sections are
declared explicitly, and the shared chrome is rebuilt to match the handoff.

**This is the visual change.** It cannot be gradual: a light header above pages
still dark is not a shippable intermediate state, even on a preview URL. That is
why the foundation shipped separately (D028).

## What changes

### The default

The document background flips to `--cream`. Sections that stay navy declare it:
the home hero, the home services band, the HR rollout section, and every closing
call-to-action band.

### Header, from `Header.dc.html`

Light and sticky: `--cream-header` at 94 percent opacity with a backdrop blur,
bottom border `--navy-08`. Logo wordmark in `--navy-text`. Nav links
`--slate-nav`, active `--gold-deeper`. Dropdown panels become white cards with
`--shadow-menu`. The language pill keeps its gold active state.

**Widen the dropdown.** `I18N-001` lengthens the consulting service name by ten
characters and the handoff sizes the panel at a 250px minimum. Do not introduce a
short variant for the navigation: the handoff itself uses three different
spellings, and the founder resolved that inconsistency deliberately (D044).

### Footer, from `Footer.dc.html`

Four columns become **five**. A `Legal` column appears carrying the legal notice,
the privacy policy and Contact; `Entreprise` shrinks to About alone; the bottom
row of legal links disappears, as does "Tous droits reserves". The bottom bar
becomes the RCCM on the left and "Abidjan, Cote d'Ivoire, 2026" on the right.
Background `--navy-footer`.

**New dictionary keys** are needed for the Legal column heading in both
languages. Remember that `Dictionary` is derived from `fr.ts`, so a missing
English key fails the build on purpose.

### Pages

Apply `.section--light` section by section, following each page's prototype.
Most content components should need no change: `UI-001` covers the global
primitives and the component-owned classes it found. Any class that still needs
covering must be added to the peritext in `global.css`, never patched locally
(D040).

Two page-level deltas the prototypes carry:

- **PCS** loses its closing call-to-action band. Hero, then footer.
- **Papillon HR Suite** alternates light, dark, light: white feature cards, the
  navy rollout section kept, white module cards.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every page matches its root prototype at 360, 768 and 1440 px in both
   languages, allowing for the six points where the repository deliberately
   diverges.
2. **`bin/contrast_sweep` returns zero failures over all pages**, with the
   foundation live rather than injected.
3. `npm run build` and `npm run check` clean.
4. New footer keys exist in both dictionaries; no internal link is broken.
5. The header dropdown holds the renamed consulting service without overflow, at
   360 and 1440 px.

## Out of scope

The Contact pages, which stay `PageScaffold` until `PAGE-002`. The copy changes
of `I18N-001`. The `LegalPage` specificity collision of `UI-FIX-001`.
