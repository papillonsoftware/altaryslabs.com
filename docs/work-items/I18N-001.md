## Goal

## The seven strings

### Hero, French

`src/i18n/fr.ts` `home.heroTitle`, currently line 106.

- from `Des solutions technologiques pour les entreprises d'Afrique.`
- to **`Votre partenaire technologique pour les entreprises Africaines.`**

`home.heroEmphasis` stays `Zones OHADA et CIMA`. **The plural is deliberate.**
*Delivery note: the key was already at that value, so no edit was made to it.*
The prototype writes "Zone" in the singular; OHADA and CIMA are two distinct
zones, and the founder confirmed the plural. Do not follow the mockup here.

### Hero, English

`src/i18n/en.ts` `home.heroTitle` and `home.heroEmphasis`, currently lines 108
and 109.

- title, from `Enterprise software built for African companies.`
  to **`Your technology partner for businesses across Africa.`**
- emphasis, from `West and Central Africa` to **`OHADA and CIMA regions`**

**The handoff did not write this line.** It changed the French hero and left the
English one on the old "Technology solutions" positioning. Shipping the French
change alone would have left the two languages arguing different things. The
English wording above was proposed and validated in the same session.

| Line | Key | To |
|---|---|---|

**One line to confirm with the founder before touching it**: `en.ts:349`,
`meta.services.description`, opens with `IT, HR and finance consulting` in lower
case. That reads as a descriptive phrase rather than as the service name, so it
may legitimately stay as it is. Ask; do not decide alone.

*Delivery note, D061: asked and answered on 2026-08-07. It is the service name,
it is capitalised, and the rule covers all three services named in that
sentence, in both languages. Two lines were therefore edited beyond the seven
listed above:*

| Line | Key | To |
|---|---|---|
| `fr.ts:350` | `meta.services.description` | `Conseil IT, RH & Corporate Finance, Développement sur mesure et solutions d'aide à la décision par IA souveraine...` |

*French keeps sentence case on `Développement sur mesure`, matching `pageName`:

would give the faulty `Développement Sur Mesure`.*

## Known risk: the header dropdown

The consulting name gains ten characters and appears in the header dropdown,
whose panel the handoff sizes at a 250px minimum. If the longer name breaks that
layout, **the fix belongs to `Header.astro`, and therefore to `UI-002`**, which
rebuilds the header entirely. Measure it, and if it breaks, record the finding
and hand it to `UI-002` rather than editing the header from this item.

*Delivery note, D062. Two things above are out of date.*

*The escape hatch is void: `UI-002` merged on 2026-08-07, before this item ran.
Nothing breaks, so nothing had to be handed anywhere, but a future reader must
not plan around a route that no longer exists.*

*The panel is not 250px. `Header.astro:213` declares `min-width: 288px`, which
is why a name this document expected to be at risk fits comfortably. Measured
across both languages at 1440, 768 and a genuinely emulated 360: the longest
label, English `IT, HR & Corporate Finance Consulting`, has an intrinsic text
width of 237px in the 242px available. One line, no wrap, no clipping, and no
horizontal overflow on any of the six pages checked.*

***The margin is 5px.*** *The next label longer than this one wraps, and so does
this one if the body font ever changes. Nothing to do today; do not lengthen a
navigation label without re-measuring.*

*The 360px figure was obtained through `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride`. A
plain window resize floors at 500px and silently reports a 360px measurement
that is really 500px, the trap already recorded in D034.*

Do not introduce a shorter variant for the navigation. That would recreate
exactly the inconsistency the founder resolved: the handoff itself uses three
different spellings across its files.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The seven strings above are changed, and no other string is.
2. `npm run build` and `npm run check` are green.
3. `routes.ts` is untouched and every internal link still resolves in both
   languages.
4. The home hero and the consulting page render correctly at 360, 768 and
   1440 px in both languages, and the header dropdown is measured.

## Why this ships on its own

These are editorial rulings with no styling dependency. Keeping them out of
`UI-001`, which deliberately changes nothing user-facing, and out of `UI-002`,
which changes everything visual, means a reviewer can check the copy against the
founder's rulings without also having to check a restyle.

Collision surface with the branches in flight is limited to `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
`UI-001` touches no dictionary file.
